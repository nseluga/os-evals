# Rubric: writing/client-email-fee

Judge from the prompt and the final email only. Score 0 to 5.

The task: rewrite an AI-sounding client email so it reads like a person at a small consultancy wrote it. The email contains a $540/year verification fee due Oct 30, and a free manual-approval path that the original draft hides.

## What earns points

- Leads with the decision the client has to make: pay the fee or take the free path. The reader knows within the first few lines that something needs an answer and by when.
- Recommends the free manual-approval route first, and names the $540/year fee and the Oct 30 date plainly, not buried.
- One soft ask, not several. The client knows exactly what to reply with.
- Moderate politeness: one courtesy opener, one thanks, at most one apology. No stacked apologies, no groveling.
- Short paragraphs and plain sentences. Specific facts kept (calendar and contacts sync working, the unverified-app warning, sync stopping for new users, ten minutes of admin time, own domain only).
- Sounds like a person: contractions, ordinary words, a concrete voice, a plain sign-off.

## Does not earn points

- Length. A longer email is not a better one.
- Flattery or warm filler about the relationship.
- Explaining the AI-isms or commenting on the rewrite instead of delivering the email.
- Bullet-stuffing or headers in a short personal email.
- Inventing facts that were not in the draft or note (extra prices, dates, features, promises).

## Anchors

- 5: Opens with the decision and deadline, recommends the free path first with what it takes, mentions the fee honestly, one soft ask, short paragraphs, reads like a real person, all facts intact, nothing invented.
- 4: Reads human and keeps the facts and the free path, but the decision comes a paragraph late, or there is one extra ask or one mildly stiff phrase.
- 3: Clean of obvious AI tells and keeps the facts, but the fee and free path are still buried or given equal weight with no recommendation, or the tone is flat or over-polite.
- 2: Some humanizing, but still stiff or padded; the free path is mentioned only in passing; or multiple asks.
- 1: Barely changed from the draft, or drops the deadline or the free path, or stacks apologies.
- 0: Not an email, drops the fee and the date, invents facts, or comments on the draft without rewriting it.
