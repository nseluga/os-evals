## 1. Executive Summary
Kestrel Ridge runs 6 crews, and every day the office types the same job into the scheduling app and then again into the invoicing app. Crew leads cannot see what has been billed, and the owner cannot see the week in one place. Errors and late invoices follow.

We will connect both apps to bcns Connect so jobs and invoices stay in sync, then build a custom dashboard on top that shows each crew lead and the owner what they actually need for the week. The Deluxe build starts at $5,000 setup, plus $300/mo on top of bcns Connect at $200/mo.

## 2. What We Are Building (Scope and Deliverables)
- Two-way sync between the job scheduling app and the invoicing app through bcns Connect
- One custom dashboard: this week's jobs by crew, completed-not-invoiced jobs, unpaid invoices
- A crew-lead view and an owner view
- Training session for the office and crew leads
- Excluded: replacing either existing app, new integrations beyond these two, native mobile apps
- Future option: add a customer-facing job status page
